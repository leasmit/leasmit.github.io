Professional Profile

I am a passionate marine mammal scientist and PhD candidate with hands-on experience in field research, data analysis, and conservation initiatives. Currently conducting research at the University of Pretoria’s Mammal Research Institute Whale Unit, I specialise in humpback whale population structure and behaviour. My expertise includes advanced molecular laboratory techniques, CATS and satellite tagging, and bioinformatics pipelines. I am a dedicated researcher, bringing a strong blend of scientific expertise, creativity, and commitment to environmental stewardship, and committed to bridging the gap between genetic data and marine conservation policy.
