# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JavaScript (Vanilla)

## Users

Academic peers, research collaborators, marine mammal scientists, conference organizers, grant committees, students, and conservation policy stakeholders (e.g., DFFE, IWC, ZSSA) seeking details on Lea Smit's research, publications, fieldwork capabilities, and curriculum vitae.

## Product Purpose

Serve as the primary professional academic portfolio for Lea Smit, PhD Candidate in Zoology at the University of Pretoria Mammal Research Institute (MRI) Whale Unit. The site provides high-credibility presentation of scientific projects, field biotelemetry and genetics methodologies, peer-reviewed dissertations, presentations, university teaching, and contact channels.

## Positioning

A rigorous, clean, and modern marine mammal bio-logging and conservation genetics portfolio rooted in empirical Southern Ocean and southern African research. Unlike generic AI templates or sterile resume listings, it presents authentic field photography, real expedition tracking, and distinction-awarded research.

## Operating Context

- Viewed on desktop browsers (scientific peer review, academic evaluation) and mobile devices (in-the-field quick sharing, conference networking).
- Used to export/print clean physical or PDF copies of the Curriculum Vitae for grant and fellowship applications.
- Deployed as a fast, lightweight, static site hostable 100% free via GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Light/Dark Mode**: Clean dual theme with persistent preference in `localStorage`.
- **Zero Heavy Frameworks**: 100% static HTML5, modern CSS3 custom properties, and lightweight vanilla JavaScript.
- **Accessibility (WCAG AA)**: Minimum 4.5:1 contrast on all body text, accessible heading hierarchy, semantic landmarks.
- **Print Optimization**: Print stylesheet tailored specifically for `cv.html` export.
- **Authentic Research Data**: Real dates, publications, degrees (Cum Laude MSc & BSc Hons), and institutional affiliations.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone**: Professional, empirical, understated, and academically distinguished.
- **Color Identity**: Deep ocean navy (`#0b1120`, `#131d31`), marine coastal blue (`#0369a1`), and pelagic teal accent (`#0f766e`).
- **Typography**: Clean, readable sans-serif system stack paired with monospaced accents for dates and technical tags.

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified academic CV of Lea Smit (University of Pretoria).
- Real fieldwork imagery from the RV *Algoa* (Voyage 306), Walker Bay small-boat tagging operations, and fluke photo-identification.
- Downloadable PDF CV document (`assets/docs/CV_Lea_Smit.pdf`).

## Product Principles

1. **Empirical Truth**: Highlight real scientific contributions, accredited institutions (UP MRI Whale Unit), and tangible conservation outputs.
2. **Speed & Accessibility**: Fast loading without heavy third-party tracking or bloated scripts; fully accessible navigation and high contrast.
3. **Clarity Over Clutter**: Generous whitespace, scannable hierarchies, and clear visual organization.
