Welcome!

I am a passionate marine mammal scientist and PhD candidate at the University of Pretoria’s Mammal Research Institute (MRI) Whale Unit, based in Hermanus, South Africa.

My research is focused on the population structure and foraging behaviour of humpback whales (Megaptera novaeangliae). By deploying high-resolution Customized Animal Tracking Solutions (CATS) camera/sensor tags and satellite biotelemetry, alongside molecular genetic analyses, I investigate how Southern Hemisphere whale populations (specifically Breeding Stocks B and C) migrate, forage, and respond to dynamic environmental changes.

I am deeply committed to bridging the gap between rigorous genetic data, field telemetry, and tangible marine conservation policy across Southern Africa and the Southern Ocean.

Research Focus

Biotelemetry

CATS & Satellite Tagging

Using multi-sensor suction-cup CATS tags and satellite telemetry to capture 3D high-resolution underwater biomechanics, kinematics, and dive behaviour during coastal migrations and feeding super-groups.

Molecular Ecology

Population Genetics

Applying microsatellite genotyping and mitochondrial DNA sequencing to resolve Southern Hemisphere humpback whale breeding stock connectivity.
