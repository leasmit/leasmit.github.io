Pelagic Cruise • 2025 RV Algoa • DFFE Voyage 306

RV Algoa Whale Cruise 2025 (Voyage 306)

Served as research scientist aboard the Department of Forestry, Fisheries, and the Environment (DFFE) research vessel RV Algoa during Voyage 306 off the South African continental shelf.

Responsibilities included systematic visual line-transect watches for cetaceans, recording behaviour and group size, deploying oceanographic monitoring gear, assisting with acoustic sonobuoy arrays, and collecting oceanographic telemetry data in offshore waters.

Coastal Operations • 2024 – Present Walker Bay, Yzerfontein & West Coast

Small-Boat Biologging & Biopsy Sampling

Conducting intensive inshore small-boat field operations aboard research vessels operating from Hermanus (Walker Bay), Yzerfontein, and along the Western and Eastern Cape coasts.

Biopsy Darting & Prey Sampling

Targeted sampling of skin and blubber tissue using specialized biopsy crossbows and dart systems for molecular analysis, prey sampling and opportunistic faecal collections.

Photo-ID

High-resolution photography of ventral fluke pigmentation and dorsal fins for individual matching and catalogue maintenance.

Tag Deployment

Assistance during suction-cup CATS tag and satellite tag deployments to track fine-scale kinematics and long-range migrations.

Stranding Response • 2026 MRI Whale Unit • Overberg Necropsy & Tissue Sampling

Marine Mammal Stranding Response & Necropsies

As part of the Mammal Research Institute Whale Unit’s stranding response network along the Western Cape coastline, I assist in emergency field responses, post-mortem examinations, morphometric documentation, and biological sample archiving. Stranding investigations provide critical baseline health, dietary, and genetic data from offshore and baleen cetaceans that are otherwise rarely accessible.

2 July 2026 'Die Plaat', Gansbaai

Fin Whale Stranding & Full Necropsy

Assisted in responding to and sampling a stranded fin whale (Balaenoptera physalus) at 'Die Plaat' in Gansbaai. Participated in a comprehensive full necropsy to investigate gross pathology and determine potential cause of death. Collected complete morphometric body measurements, baleen plates for chronological and isotopic growth analyses, and dermal skin and blubber samples for genetic profiling and biomarker investigation.

21 August 2026 Overberg Coastline

Southern Right Whale Calf Stranding

Helped respond to and attend a 6-metre southern right whale (Eubalaena australis) calf that washed ashore. Conducted standardized carcass assessment and morphometric body measurements, collected baleen plates to evaluate maternal transfer and nursing status, and sampled skin and blubber tissue for population genetics and condition assessment.
