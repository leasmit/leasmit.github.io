University Teaching & Tutoring

University Tutor • Department of Genetics

University of Pretoria, South Africa

2023 – 2024

Tutored first- and third-year undergraduate students in molecular biology, genetics, and evolutionary theory across two core academic modules:

Introductory Genetics (1st Year)

Guided students through Mendelian genetics, DNA replication, gene expression, and problem-solving workshops for large undergraduate cohorts.

Genome Evolution & Phylogenetics (3rd Year)

Facilitated computational and practical modules on sequence alignments, phylogenetic tree estimation, molecular clocks, and genome divergence.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Prepared and delivered weekly practical coursework materials and tutorial sessions.
  • Broke down complex scientific and mathematical concepts into accessible explanations.
  • Graded semester tests, continuous assessments, and university examinations.
  • Provided one-on-one academic mentoring to foster student success in STEM.

Conservation Outreach & Public Engagement

Bridging Marine Science with Community Awareness

Science is most impactful when shared beyond academic journals. Throughout my research and internships (including Oceans Research in Mossel Bay and the MRI Whale Unit in Hermanus), I have actively engaged with the public, ecotourism operators, and local schools.

Topics include whale migration ecology, how community members can participate in citizen science (such as fluke photo-ID via Happywhale), responsible boat-based marine wildlife watching, and local marine pollution mitigation through initiatives like Dive Against Debris.
