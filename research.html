Doctoral Research • 2026 – Present MRI Whale Unit • University of Pretoria

Humpback Whale Population Structure, Biotelemetry & Foraging Behaviour

Southern Hemisphere humpback whales (Megaptera novaeangliae) undertake extensive annual migrations between tropical winter breeding grounds and productive polar summer feeding grounds in the Southern Ocean. Along the southern African coastline, complex migratory pathways and high-density feeding aggregations ("super-groups") challenge historical stock management boundaries.

My doctoral research at the Mammal Research Institute Whale Unit investigates fine-scale population structure, movement dynamics, and underwater foraging behaviour. By deploying high-resolution Customized Animal Tracking Solutions (CATS) multi-sensor camera/biologging tags and satellite telemetry alongside molecular genetics, I study 3D underwater biomechanics, dive profiles, kinematics, and environmental drivers during coastal migrations and feeding events.

Master's Research • 2024 – 2025 Cum Laude MRI Whale Unit • University of Pretoria

Humpback Whale Population Genetics, Stock Connectivity & Movement Dynamics

My Master of Science research at the Mammal Research Institute Whale Unit (awarded Cum Laude) investigated the molecular ecology, population genetic connectivity, and movement patterns of Southern Hemisphere humpback whales (Megaptera novaeangliae) across Southern Africa.

A central focus of this research evaluated genetic connectivity and gene flow between International Whaling Commission (IWC) Breeding Stock B (Southeast Atlantic / West Coast) and Breeding Stock C (Southwest Indian Ocean / East Coast). By applying multi-locus microsatellite genotyping and mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) control region sequencing, I resolved population differentiation, genetic diversity, and migratory connectivity between these regional breeding stocks along the southern African coastline.

In addition, I investigated the stock composition and demographic structure of humpback whale feeding "super-groups"—extraordinary aggregations of up to 200+ individuals that congregate off the South African West Coast during the austral spring and summer. Using molecular assignment testing and genetic profiling, I evaluated whether these feeding assemblages draw animals from Stock B, Stock C, or reflect temporary mixing of multiple stocks.

The study also documented individual whale movements between the South African West Coast and East Coast, alongside broader spatial connectivity to sub-Antarctic and Southern Ocean feeding areas, providing critical empirical data for Southern African marine spatial planning and international whale conservation.

Honours Research • 2023 Cum Laude Department of Genetics • University of Pretoria

BSc Honours Research: Molecular Genetics & Evolutionary Genomics

Completed within the Department of Genetics at the University of Pretoria and awarded with distinction (Cum Laude), my Bachelor of Science Honours research provided comprehensive training in molecular genetics, evolutionary theory, and computational bioinformatics pipelines.

The research encompassed rigorous molecular bench protocols, including high-molecular-weight genomic DNA extractions, target-specific PCR amplification, agarose gel electrophoresis, microsatellite genotyping calibration, and Sanger sequencing workflows.

Computationally, I implemented phylogenetic and phylogenomic analysis pipelines, investigating sequence variation, gene evolution, and phylogenetic tree reconstruction using dedicated evolutionary and statistical software packages, including MEGA, CLC Genomics Workbench, and custom R scripts. This solid foundation in molecular techniques and evolutionary genomics directly paved the way for my postgraduate cetacean population genetics research at the MRI Whale Unit.

Methods & Analytical Toolkit

Field Biotelemetry

Suction-cup CATS tag deployment via carbon fiber pole, satellite tag deployment, biopsy darting sampling, high-resolution photo-identification (Happywhale matching), and prey/faecal sample collection.

Computation & Modeling

Statistical programming in R, sensor kinematic data calibration and processing in MATLAB, geospatial mapping in QGIS, and reproducible scripting in Bash.

Conservation Integration

Collaborating with national and international stakeholders (Department of Forestry, Fisheries and the Environment, Whales & Climate Program) to apply empirical behavioural findings to marine spatial planning.
