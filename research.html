Humpback Whale Population Structure & Behaviour

Southern Hemisphere humpback whales (Megaptera novaeangliae) undertake extensive annual migrations between tropical winter breeding grounds and productive polar summer feeding grounds in the Southern Ocean. Along the southern African coastline, complex migratory paths and uncharacteristic high-density feeding aggregations ("super-groups") challenge historical stock definitions.

My doctoral research at the Mammal Research Institute Whale Unit combines high-resolution biologging and molecular genetics to resolve the fine-scale population structure, behavioural flexibility, and demographic connectivity of humpback whales utilizing South African waters.

Biotelemetry & Kinematics CATS Technology

CATS Tagging & High-Resolution Bio-logging

Customized Animal Tracking Solutions (CATS) tags represent the gold standard in non-invasive cetacean bio-logging. Attached via gentle suction cups, these tags record continuous 3-axis acceleration, magnetometer, gyroscope, pressure (depth), and forward-facing HD video.

Through sensor calibration, dead-reckoning, and pseudotracking in MATLAB, we reconstruct continuous 3D underwater dive profiles. This enables us to quantify stroke frequency, pitch-roll-yaw body orientation, lunge feeding kinematics, and energy expenditure during feeding super-group events.

Molecular Laboratory Conservation Genetics

Molecular Ecology & Bioinformatics Pipelines

Biopsy darting yields skin and blubber samples that provide essential genetic and hormonal data. In the molecular laboratory, I conduct genomic DNA extractions, PCR amplification, gel electrophoresis, and multi-locus microsatellite genotyping alongside mitochondrial DNA control region sequencing.

Downstream analyses utilize dedicated population genetic software (Cervus, Arlequin) and custom R pipelines to quantify genetic diversity, evaluate gene flow between coastal breeding stocks, assign individuals, and detect kinship within feeding groups.

Methods & Analytical Toolkit

Field Biotelemetry

Suction-cup CATS tag deployment via carbon fiber pole, satellite tag deployment, biopsy darting sampling, high-resolution photo-identification (Happywhale matching), and prey/faecal sample collection.

Computation & Modeling

Statistical programming in R, sensor kinematic data calibration and processing in MATLAB, geospatial mapping in QGIS, and reproducible scripting in Bash.

Conservation Integration

Collaborating with national and international stakeholders (Department of Forestry, Fisheries and the Environment, Whales & Climate Program) to apply empirical behavioural findings to marine spatial planning.
